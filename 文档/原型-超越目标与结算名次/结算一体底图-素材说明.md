## 预制件和动态布局

- 基准从 750×1488 收紧为 750×1360；底图显示框在 (0,56)，分数、奖励和榜单都在同一块内容区。
- 普通路径移除旧 Title Label，只有无渲染组件的 TitleBounds 记录标题彩纸保护区；加载失败时的基础回退仍有文字标题。
- 得分字框 570×96，字号 76，单行 SHRINK，不随位数扩大。字体为项目 EazyChatR.ttf，颜色为珊瑚橙。最高合成和金币收成一行，取消两块小签。
- 最高合成图标按本局数值动态显示数字、底色和轮廓，与棋盘共同读取 PieceStyle；例如 512 使用紫色。标题字框修为 104×58，图标与标题之间留 6 设计单位，修复原先 15 单位的重叠。复用既有棋子位图，不新增 PNG；外侧数值与小棋子均绑定同一本局最高值。
- 实际字体字宽测算：`1,258,880` 为 339.97，`999,999,999` 为 425.81，`1,000,000,000` 为 535.77；加细描边仍在 570 的字框内。该测算基于 FreeType，不代替 Creator 的字体基线与缩放验收。
- 榜单 Mask 632×500，行 608×90、节距 96；八个对手槽和一个本人槽。自己冲榜时头像、名字、分数与行底同步移动，五行落位不被头部素材占用。
- 其他玩家只用轻淡分隔线；本人沿用 #F58155 与细暖棕轮廓、少量彩铅短线。
- 首页 126×126、重玩 126×126、分享 316×126；按钮图片及 UUID 保留，只调整整组位置。
- 冲榜、彩纸、数据归属和生命周期保持既有逻辑；本轮不改后端和 main，不写入演示数据。

## ImageGen 完整提示词

### 提取单体底图

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit / game UI asset extraction and clean rebuild.
Input image is the APPROVED final game screenshot. Extract and rebuild ONLY its single combined settlement card artwork as a production PNG sprite. This is NOT another screenshot.
One integrated object: a light cream rectangular panel with slim honey-gold ornamental rim and small curled corner accents; its top edge is overlapped by a compact coral ribbon with folded ends, and ONE small golden crown centered above the ribbon. Match the approved object's shape, proportions, warm outlines, matte crayon art and light visual weight closely. Header crown + ribbon and panel are ONE integrated asset, not separate repeated decoration.

OUTPUT: isolated object on TRUE TRANSPARENT background, straight-on orthographic, canvas target 720 wide x 1112 high, no perspective. The full combined crown-to-bottom panel fills the canvas with about 12px transparent safety padding at its widest edges and 10px at top/bottom. The card cream body starts about 120px below the top of the canvas; the small crown occupies about the first 70px; the coral ribbon is centered around y125 and about 480px wide, 100px high. Keep a large completely blank coral central ribbon face for a separate text label.
Card outline warm brown #62391F, thin honey gold #F6B43A rim, one simple ochre shadow edge. Slim small curls in four corners; no bulky 3D layers. Top corners meet the ribbon gracefully. Crown is petite, not broad or dominant. No leaves or flowers.

CRITICAL RUNTIME REQUIREMENTS:
- Remove ALL lettering and data from the screenshot object. NO title characters, NO score, NO reward captions, NO icons, NO medals, NO avatars, NO rows, NO separators, NO rank digits, NO confetti. Not even ghost lettering. The coral ribbon must be completely blank.
- Entire cream interior must be a SINGLE SOLID color exactly #FFF9ED, completely opaque alpha255, with NO paper texture, hatching, gradient, glow, vignette, shadows or semitransparent pixels. This same clean surface extends under where score, rewards and animated list will be drawn by the game. Do not pre-draw their placeholders.
- Restrained dry-crayon strokes are allowed ONLY on coral ribbon and crown. Matte cartoon, simple two-tone flat painted edge shadow. No white highlight lines, no shiny gold, no metal/plastic, no photorealism, no blur.
- Everything outside the combined card/crown/ribbon silhouette is genuinely alpha transparent, no scene, no sky, no white rectangle, no checkerboard painted into image.
- No trophy, laurel, animals, flowers, interior artwork, decorative filler or any item in the interior. The free interior hosts a moving leaderboard, so it must be empty and clean.
Return ONE clean production settlement panel sprite, no contact sheet, no labels, no watermark.
```
