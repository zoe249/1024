## 生成提示词

工具：内置 ImageGen，两次调用，第二次以第一次生成结果为编辑输入，两次均要求透明背景。

初次生成：

> Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: one production transparent PNG circular avatar BORDER for a playful hand-painted Cocos mobile number game. Create ONLY a perfectly round, flat, narrow annular frame, no avatar and no photo. Square 256x256 target canvas. Centered frontal circle, no perspective or tilt. Outside diameter 224px, inner opening diameter 184px at target resolution, evenly weighted and concentric, transparent center and transparent outside. Frame has a very thin muted warm orange outer rim (#E57835), and a slightly broader solid warm cream band (#FFF7DA) inside. Optional subtle flat darker orange edge only, absolutely no white highlight, no gloss, no bevel, no shadow or halo, no texture/noise, no flowers/leaves/crowns/decorations, no text, no numbers. It must feel light and clean, matching a charming illustrated game UI while geometrically smooth. It will appear around an avatar at an effective outside diameter of 40 screen pixels, so keep the silhouette extremely smooth and the color bands simple. High quality antialiased alpha on both the outer contour and inner opening; all opaque interior band pixels alpha 255, all center and exterior pixels alpha 0 except narrow edge antialiasing. Single isolated frame only. Genuine transparent background, not a checkerboard drawing.

定稿修正：

> Edit target: the supplied transparent circular border asset. Keep ONLY one centered perfect circle, fully transparent center and outside, no other elements. Important production correction: make the circular band about 45% thicker towards the center while keeping the same outside diameter. The INNER OPENING DIAMETER must be approximately 80% of the OUTSIDE DIAMETER, a thick enough band to overlap an avatar clipping edge. At a 256x256 target canvas, outer diameter 224px and inner opening about 180px. The ring still looks light at 40px display size: use ONE thin flat orange outer rim (#E57835), and ONE uniform solid cream inner band (#FFF7DA). Remove the orange line along the inner opening. Absolutely uniform flat colors: NO gradient, NO highlight, NO shading, NO glow, NO noise, NO speckles, NO drawing texture, NO bevel, NO shadow. Both circular edges need smooth high quality antialiased alpha. All band interior pixels alpha 255. All center/exterior alpha 0 except the narrow smooth silhouette antialiasing. No avatar, photo, text, numbers, flowers, jewels or decorations. Genuine transparent PNG.
