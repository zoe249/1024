# 设置窗口设计稿 v2

## 功能与状态规则

- 常驻内容：音效滑块、音量滑块、转发好友、客服反馈、关闭按钮。
- 首页状态：不显示“返回首页”和“重新开始”。
- 游戏中状态：在常驻内容下方增加单独的游戏操作区，显示“返回首页”和“重新开始”。
- “转发好友”和“客服反馈”使用轻量的奶油色按钮；游戏操作使用更醒目的黄色与珊瑚橙按钮，降低误认概率。

## 首页状态提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit. Upgrade the supplied homepage settings-popup mockup while preserving its background, dark-teal overlay, title, close button, two sliders, colors, hand-drawn crayon texture and low-glare material. Below the “音量” slider add a thin warm-brown separator and exactly two equal compact rounded utility buttons side by side. Left: coral-orange share/network icon with label exactly “转发好友”. Right: warm-yellow headset/speech-bubble icon with label exactly “客服反馈”. Text exactly once: “设置”, “音效”, “音量”, “转发好友”, “客服反馈”. This is the HOME state: do not add “返回首页” or “重新开始”. No extra buttons, toggles, checkboxes, English, watermark, glossy plastic or cropped panel.

## 游戏中状态提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit. Upgrade the supplied gameplay settings-popup mockup while preserving the gameplay board, dark-teal pause overlay, title, close button, two sliders and low-glare hand-drawn crayon style. Organize the popup into three vertical groups: first the unchanged “音效” and “音量” sliders; second, after a thin separator, two compact permanent utility buttons, “转发好友” with a coral share icon and “客服反馈” with a warm-yellow headset icon; third, after another separator, two larger conditional game actions, “返回首页” with a white house icon and “重新开始” with a white circular-arrow icon. Keep the popup fully visible and touch-friendly. Text exactly once: “设置”, “音效”, “音量”, “转发好友”, “客服反馈”, “返回首页”, “重新开始”. The last two controls appear only during gameplay. No extra buttons, toggles, checkboxes, English, watermark, glossy plastic or cropped panel.
