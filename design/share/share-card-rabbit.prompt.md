# 微信分享卡片素材 · 兔子 1024 版

## 用途

微信小游戏 `wx.shareAppMessage` 的横版分享图。运行时通过 `resources/Share/share-card-rabbit` 加载并传入 `imageUrl`。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: production-ready WeChat mini-game share-card artwork, horizontal 5:4.
Replace only the brown bear with a cheerful white rabbit with long upright ears, pink inner ears, rosy cheeks and the same blue scarf. The rabbit holds a rounded green tile labeled exactly “1024” with both paws and smiles normally. Remove the bear and the dandelion it held.
Preserve the blue crayon sky, clouds, meadow, flowers, corner leaves, blue bird, logo “1024 数字花园”, slogan “一起合成 1024 吧！”, equation “512 + 512 → 1024”, caption “看看你能合成多高?”, positions, colors, paper texture, thick brown outlines and safe margins.
Output only the card artwork. No phone, WeChat interface, QR code, frame, bear, blowing gesture, garbled Chinese, wrong numbers or watermark.
```

## 文件

- `share-card-rabbit-source.png`：内置图像生成的高分辨率源图。
- `../../assets/resources/Share/share-card-rabbit.png`：1000 × 800 px 运行时素材。
